Understanding bipolar

Bipolar involves shifts in mood, energy and activity that go beyond everyday ups and downs. These states aren't choices or character flaws — they're part of a complex, treatable condition. Learning your own patterns is a powerful form of self-knowledge.

The mood spectrum
LowStableElevated
Depression

Low mood and energy, sleep changes, withdrawal. Small tasks can feel overwhelming.

Stable

Balanced mood, steady energy, clear thinking — the ground most treatment aims to protect.

Hypomania

Elevated mood, more energy and ideas, talking more. Milder than mania, but still a shift.

Mania

Racing thoughts, very little sleep, impulsivity or euphoria. Can lead to risky decisions.

Living with bipolar

Symptom lists don't capture everything. These are some of the quieter realities people live with.
